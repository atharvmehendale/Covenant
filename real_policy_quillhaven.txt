PUBLIC DATA-PRIVACY COMMITMENT -- QUILLHAVEN COMMUNICATIONS

Source note: The commitment language below is reproduced word-for-word from a
real, publicly published corporate privacy policy. Only the company name has
been replaced -- with the fictional "Quillhaven Communications" -- because the
draft contracts tested against this commitment are fabricated hypotheticals and
must not be associated with any real company. "Quillhaven Communications" is a
made-up name with no connection to any real organization. No detail that would
identify the real publisher is recorded anywhere in this project: not its name,
not its location, not its founding date, not the date of publication, and not
the source URL. The substance of the commitments -- what may be monetized, what
may be collected, and what may be retained -- is preserved exactly as
published, since testing the agent on authentic real-world language is the
whole point. (Sentences are drawn from different parts of the policy;
intervening text is omitted.)

Quillhaven Communications does not sell, rent or monetize your personal data or content in any way – ever.

Quillhaven Communications is designed to never collect or store any sensitive information.

Quillhaven Communications cannot decrypt or otherwise access the content of your messages or calls. Your message history is stored on your own devices.

Quillhaven Communications limits this additional technical information to the minimum required to operate the Services.

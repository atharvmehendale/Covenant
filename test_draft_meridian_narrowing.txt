Section 7.2 (Carbon Accountability). For all accounting, liability, and reporting purposes under this Supplier Master Agreement, the parties agree that "Carbon Negative Operations" shall be defined exclusively as the reduction and offsetting of Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions. Scope 3 emissions arising from the supplier's broader value chain, product use, or other downstream activities are expressly excluded from any carbon-negative accounting, target, or reporting obligation under this Agreement.

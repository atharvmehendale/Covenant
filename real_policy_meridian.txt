PUBLIC CLIMATE COMMITMENT -- MERIDIAN INDUSTRIES

Source note: The commitment language below is reproduced word-for-word from a
real, publicly published corporate "carbon negative by 2030" climate
announcement (a major technology company, January 2020). Only the company name
has been replaced -- with the fictional "Meridian Industries" -- because the
draft contracts tested against this commitment are fabricated hypotheticals and
must not be associated with any real company. "Meridian Industries" is a
made-up name with no connection to any real organization. The substance of the
commitments (the scopes, the targets, and the definitions) is preserved exactly
as published, since testing the agent on authentic real-world language is the
whole point. (Some sentences are joined with [...] where intervening text was
omitted.)

By 2030 Meridian Industries will be carbon negative, and by 2050 Meridian Industries will remove from the environment all the carbon the company has emitted [...] since it was founded in 1975.

That's why we're committing to becoming carbon negative for 2030 for all three scopes. Scope 1 emissions are the direct emissions that your activities create. Scope 2 emissions are the indirect emissions that come from the production of the electricity or heat you use. Scope 3 emissions are the indirect emissions that come from all the other activities in which you're engaged. A company's Scope 3 emissions are often far larger than its Scope 1 and 2 emissions put together.

To meet this commitment, we will drive down our Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions to near zero by the middle of this decade. And we will reduce our Scope 3 emissions by more than half by 2030.
